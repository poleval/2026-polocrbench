II Kąty trójkąta pozostają w stosunku 1:2:4. Wykazać, że między bokami zachodzi związek

$$\frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{a} - \frac{1}{c}$$

$$\alpha : \beta : \gamma = 1:2:4$$

$$\frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{a} - \frac{1}{c} \quad ?$$

$$a = \frac{b \sin\alpha}{\sin\beta} \qquad c = \frac{b \sin\gamma}{\sin\beta}$$

$$\frac{1}{b} \stackrel{?}{=} \frac{\sin\beta}{b \sin\alpha} - \frac{\sin\beta}{b \sin\gamma}$$

$$1 \stackrel{?}{=} \frac{\sin\beta}{\sin\frac{\beta}{2}} - \frac{\sin\beta}{\sin 2\beta}$$

$$2\cos\frac{\beta}{2} - \frac{1}{2\cos\beta} \stackrel{?}{=} 1$$

$$\beta = \frac{2}{7} 180^{\circ} = 51^{\circ}26'$$

$$\frac{\beta}{2} = 25^{\circ}43'$$

$$2\cos 25^{\circ}43' - \frac{1}{2\cos 51^{\circ}26'} \stackrel{?}{=} 1$$

$$2 \cdot 0,9009 - \frac{1}{2 \cdot 0,6234}$$

$$1,8018 - \frac{1}{1,2468}$$

$$1,8018 - 0,802 \approx 1$$
